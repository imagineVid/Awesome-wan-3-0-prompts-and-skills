# FAQ

## What is this repository?

It is an ImagineVid-maintained collection of source-verifiable Wan 3.0 Video prompts, playable results, and production notes. Every published entry retains its creator, canonical source, model evidence, and playable result.

## What does the collection document?

It documents patterns visible in public Wan 3.0 demonstrations and community workflows: camera paths, shot pacing, reference-led continuity, native audio cues, commercial presentation, stylized action, and scene control. A community post is evidence for that post, not a blanket specification sheet for the model.

## Where can I try the workflow?

The current ImagineVid entry point is the supported [text-to-video workspace](https://imaginevid.io/text-to-video); localized READMEs use the matching locale route. Product access and model availability can change, so confirm the current experience before production use.

## How are prompts selected?

Community cases enter the collection only after source verification, model confirmation, video review, workflow classification, and duplicate checks. Showcase-only posts, image-only results, and prompts without attributable results are excluded.

## How does localization work?

English remains the source language. Each localized README translates the editorial guidance and provides a reviewed prompt translation while keeping the creator's original English prompt visible for provenance and reuse.

## What makes a video prompt reusable?

A reusable brief usually names the subject and action, gives the camera a path, defines pacing, states the intended sound or dialogue, identifies reference inputs, and says what must remain stable. Image-to-video prompts should separate anchor-frame constraints from motion instructions.
